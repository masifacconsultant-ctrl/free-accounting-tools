# Free Accounting Tools

Free, browser-based tools for bookkeepers and accountants, built by [CoController AI](https://getcocontroller.com). These tools run entirely in your browser — the converters and calculators below process files locally on your device, so your client data is never uploaded to a server.

## The tools

### Bank Statement Converter
Turn a bank statement PDF into a clean, import-ready file for QuickBooks Online — no more retyping statement lines by hand.
https://getcocontroller.com/free-bank-statement-converter

### Sales Tax Nexus Tracker
Track your sales by state against each state's economic nexus thresholds, with free guides for California, Texas and Florida.
https://getcocontroller.com/free-nexus-tracker

### Bookkeeping Pricing Calculator
Work out what to charge for bookkeeping services based on the work involved, so quotes stop being guesswork.
https://getcocontroller.com/free-bookkeeping-pricing-calculator

## About

CoController AI is an AI coworker for the month-end close in QuickBooks Online — bank-feed categorisation prepared for review, fixed assets, prepayments and accruals, and the payroll journal, with an accountant approving everything before anything posts. It works under US GAAP, IFRS and UK GAAP (FRS 102).
